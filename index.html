Procedure

  1. Intake

    Tapes and discs are logged by title, label and length before anything is touched.

  2. Digitize

    Each source is transferred to a digital master so the original never has to play again.

  3. Identify

    Footage is broken into scenes and matched to dates, opponents and scores using period newspapers.

  4. Restore & cut

    Light cleanup of tape noise, then a titled film for each event, with sourced score and credit cards.

  5. Deliver

    Finished films are shared privately, such as unlisted video links, with the digital masters kept safe.
