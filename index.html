About

DKF69FILMS is a film and video production label rooted in the Adirondacks. The catalog begins with restored local pictures: the 1985 Saranac Lake JV football season, and marching band performances from the same school community.

Work released under this label carries the closing credit an ADKDon Production. This page will be updated as those transfers go up on YouTube.
